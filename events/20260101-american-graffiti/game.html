📌 Welcome to Modesto, September 1962

Four high-school buddies are about to spend one last night cruising the main drag before college — and the whole story is told on a visual deck called Deconstructing American Graffiti: The Last Great Night of Youth. This arcade turns that deck into five machines: repair the marquee sentences, judge the Wolfman’s broadcast, pick the right road, pair the chrome vocabulary, and survive the bonus reel at full throttle.

Every reel teaches the deck’s real vocabulary and facts — from “cold feet” and “a crown that is slipping” to diegetic soundtracks and the $777,000 gamble. Play the reels in any order you like, as many times as you like; your best results are saved in this browser.
