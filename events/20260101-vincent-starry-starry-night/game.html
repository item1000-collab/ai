Starry Night Arcade 🌌

One song, one painting, four lives

In 1971, a broke folk singer named Don McLean read a book by Theo van Gogh and wrote “Vincent (Starry, Starry Night)” — a single, emotional acoustic take for a painter the world refused to hear. A century later it became the favorite song of Tupac Shakur, playing in a hospital room. This arcade retells that chain of empathy in five reels, and every reel drills the vocabulary of the lesson: lament, unrequited, solace, elusive…

Play the reels in any order — the map never locks a door.
