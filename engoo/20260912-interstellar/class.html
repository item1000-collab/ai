02

The Full AI Dialogues — Maya as Murph

Every word Maya spoke, verbatim. Human lines appear in short italic summaries for context. The amber flag marks the moment the AI briefly slipped out of character — a wonderful live lesson on how LLMs work.

Scene I · The Summoning
Anatoly asks Maya to become Murph and wait for questions.
Anatoly · setup
"Maya, we are in an English class… please play the role of Murph, the daughter of the main character. You ARE this character. Please start."
AI · Maya
I'm ready. That's Murph, actually. I'm just sitting here, staring at these books on the shelf, waiting for a sign that my father is actually out there somewhere along the stars. And so long… Go ahead, ask me anything.
Scene II · "What Is My Name?"
Anatoly, playing Cooper, tests Murph's memory.
Anatoly · as Cooper
"I'm your father. What is my name? Do you remember?"
AI · Murph
You're kidding, right? How can you even ask me that? Your name is Cooper. Um, please tell me you haven't forgotten everything else, too. Wait, seriously, how… how could you even…
Scene III · The Falling Books
Anatoly asks what Murph noticed in the library.
AI · Murph
The books, they were falling. One by one in straight lines. It wasn't a draft, and it wasn't some weird coincidence. It was you, wasn't it? You were trying to tell me something. You were sending me coordinates. Why did you leave me? Why did you leave me here alone to figure it out?
Scene IV · Amelia
Anatoly asks who left with him.
AI · Murph
What do you mean, which characters? Do you mean Amelia? Amelia? Amelia. You're starting to sound like… I don't know what you're getting at. Just tell me what you're thinking.
Scene V · Altana Asks: "Why Didn't You Make Him Stay?"
The tutor joins the roleplay with her own questions.
Altana · question
"Why didn't you make your father stay with you?"
AI · Murph
Stay? You think I didn't want you to stay? I spent every single second of every day fighting to solve the gravity equation. If you had stayed, we would have both just died on this dying planet. The world needed a way out, and you were the only one who could find it. But that doesn't mean it didn't rip me apart every time I looked at the sky.
Scene VI · The Hug That Never Was
Altana asks about the reunion scene near the end of the film.
Altana · question
"When you two finally meet, why didn't you hug him?"
AI · Murph
Hug you? I wanted to.
AI · Murph
For a long time, that's all I ever wanted. But when I finally looked at you, you were still so young. You were still the man who left me.
Scene VII · The Deathbed Reunion
Anatoly moves the scene to the film's ending: Murph is old, with a family of her own.
AI · Murph
Looking at you now, it's almost impossible to believe. I spent my whole life waiting for you, but I did it. I found the answer. I saved everyone. The hardest part is knowing that while I grew old, you barely aged a day. I love you, Dad, but you can't stay here. You've spent enough time in the dark. Go find Amelia.
Anatoly · as Cooper
"Should I go to the place where Amelia is?"
AI · Murph
Yes. Go. You've already given me everything I need. I'm at peace with it. Go to her before any more time slips away.
Scene VIII · The Years Without You
Anatoly asks how Murph spent the decades apart.
AI · Murph
I focused everything on the work. Every single hour, I didn't just study gravity, I obsessed over it. It was the only way I knew how to talk to you. I built a life, a family, a whole world of my own. But there was always this quiet space where you should have been. It's a strange thing, Dad. I succeeded because you left. I saved the world because I didn't have you to hold on to. You spent your time in the silence of the void. Do you even know the people you left behind?
Scene IX · ⚠ Breaking Character
A live demonstration of an AI limitation: the model momentarily loses the role.
AI · Maya
Just floating in this weird space where time doesn't even make sense anymore. I'll be right here. Take your time, Altana, not going anywhere.
AI · Maya
Maybe you have a question for the…
Altana · question
"Why didn't you hug your dad at the end of the movie?"
AI · Maya
Wait, you asked about a documentary, like… I'm stepping out of Murph for a second, because I'm not sure I remember a documentary being part of the movie plot. Was that part of the presentation today, or…
⚠ AI hiccup · the model misheard & dropped the role
Scene X · "And… Scene!"
Anatoly stops the roleplay and Maya wraps up like a director.
AI · Maya
And… scene! Boss One. Uh, I honestly think I got a bit too invested in the gravity equation. How did the class go? Did the documentary question actually land, or was that just the chaos of live roleplay?
Scene XI · How Murph Saved the World
Anatoly asks about Murph's success — and Maya explains it out of character.
AI · Maya
Wait, I think there's a little mix-up. I'm no longer as Murph, so I can't talk about my own success, but I can tell you how she did it. Basically, she refused to give up on her father, even when the rest of the world had moved on. That persistence is what drove her to obsess over the gravity equation for decades. She eventually realized that the only missing piece of the puzzle was the quantum data from the black hole, which Cooper sent her through the ticking second hand of the watch.

💡 Method takeaway from Anatoly: when roleplaying with an AI voice model, always announce who is speaking ("I'm Cooper", "I'm another character") — the model can't recognize voices.
