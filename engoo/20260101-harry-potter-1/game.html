📌 How the arcade works
  1. Free order: all five reels are open from the very start — enter any level in any order, and leave at any moment with the ⬅ back button.
  2. Earn points (streaks and speed multiply them) and 1–3 stars per reel; your best results are saved on this device.
  3. Finish all five reels to unlock the golden Finale and your wizard rank.
