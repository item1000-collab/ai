📖 Summary

This lesson centered on the sitcom Friends, season two, episode three. The teacher and student explored three main plot lines: the inheritance of odd items from Mr. Heckles, the dispute between Monica and Rachel over a tacky seashell lamp, and the debate between Ross and Phoebe about evolution. The discussion highlighted character dynamics, comedic tension, and the cultural backdrop of 1990s New York.

Key vocabulary such as eclectic, devastated, superficial, incremental and the idiom "you break it, you buy it" were examined. The student also incorporated AI-generated role-play to deepen the conversation, acting as Rachel and Ross. The session emphasized understanding character motivations, thematic contrasts (conservative vs. free-spirited), and the show's use of humor to address deeper human insecurities.
