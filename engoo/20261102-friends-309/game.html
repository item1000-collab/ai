📼 The story so far

It is a rainy Tuesday in New York. Ross carries a broken heart and a box of records into Central Perk. Rachel bursts through the same door in a wedding dress, leaving her almost-marriage behind. Monica turns cooking into therapy, Chandler turns everything into a joke, Joey turns charm into a career plan, and Phoebe turns every café conversation into cosmic wisdom.

Twenty words and expressions from their world are recorded on the five reels of this arcade. Phrases like take a rain check, come clean and get cold feet hide inside sentences, statements, questions and pairs. By the final rewind they will feel like old friends.
