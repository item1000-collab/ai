Three Nights at Barich

The marble palace above the river Susta has a new tenant. The tenant has three nights.

“Pass the day there, if you like, but never stay the night.” — Karim Khan, the old clerk

🪔 Two forces will fill as you choose: the Hunger of the Stones and the Grip of the Mirage. If either reaches one hundred, the palace keeps you.

🎀 Three fragments of her — the golden frill, the wrought bodice, the dark eyes — are hidden along the paths. Some players see all of her; some see almost nothing.

🌅 Five endings exist. The dawn train is the honest one. The rest, the palace decides.
