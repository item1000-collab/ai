📚 Target vocabulary & idioms

to spill the beans — accidentally reveal a secret (Chandler spills the beans about Ross’s feelings).
to pull the rug out (from under) — suddenly take away support or change the situation; the ending pulled the rug out from under Rachel.
oblivious (to) — not aware of something; Rachel is oblivious to Ross’s romantic feelings.
pining (for) — to miss or long for someone who is far away; Ross is pining for Rachel.
cliffhanger — a dramatic ending that leaves the audience in suspense; the finale is a television cliffhanger.
antique pin — a valuable vintage brooch; Ross gives Rachel an antique pin she once admired.

📖 Summary

In this engaging English lesson, Teacher Mayen and student Anatoliy explore the season 1 finale of the iconic sitcom Friends. They analyse key plot points: Rachel’s birthday, Ross’s trip to China, Chandler’s accidental revelation, and the shocking cliffhanger when Ross returns with a new girlfriend. The lesson integrates a student‑created AI presentation, which structures vocabulary, character dynamics, and discussion prompts, while also featuring a live role‑play with an AI chatbot impersonating Rachel.

The session highlights advanced expressions such as “spill the beans,” “pull the rug out,” and “oblivious,” while also delving into the emotional complexity of the Rachel‑Ross relationship. By combining authentic dialogue, AI interaction, and targeted vocabulary, the lesson offers a modern, immersive approach to learning English through pop culture. Teacher Mayen and Anatoliy also exchange creative interests—writing and blogging—fostering a collaborative and personalised learning environment.
