Five playable reels built page by page on the Roman Holiday retrospective deck — fill the blanks, fact-check the delusions, match the meanings, and face the Mouth of Truth.

Princess Ann slips her royal prison for twenty-four hours of freedom, and a cynical reporter named Joe Bradley decides a hidden princess is worth a $5,000 scoop — or maybe worth far more than money. This arcade rebuilds that one perfect day as five reels, each drawn from a page of the lesson deck: the escape, the morning press game, the Vespa chaos, the editor's desk, and the stone mouth that bites the hands of liars.

Play the reels in any order, keep the stars you earn, and finish all five to open the finale. Progress saves automatically on this device, so the story waits patiently for your return.
