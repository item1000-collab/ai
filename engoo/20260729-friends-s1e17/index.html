🎭 Thematic blocks

❄️ Seasons & culture

Four seasons vs. perpetual summer: Katarina prefers the changing seasons; Christmas without snow feels wrong. Anatoly mentions Russian tourists in Vietnam during winter — a cultural exchange of climate.

🎬 Pop culture & George Clooney

ER nostalgia & Friends cameo: Clooney’s role as a doctor in ER made his Friends appearance iconic. The audience recognised him instantly. Also discussed: Up in the Air — a film about business travel and isolation.

🔄 Twin triangle · Phoebe & Ursula

Joey & the twin switch: Joey is in love with Ursula (Phoebe’s twin). Ursula stands him up; Phoebe pretends to be Ursula to let him down gently. A masterclass in acting by Lisa Kudrow.

📄 Insurance fraud plot

Identity swap: Rachel and Monica swap names to use Monica’s insurance for Rachel’s pet. The hospital catches on — a mix of comedy and a real crime (insurance fraud).

📚 Advanced vocabulary & idioms
to stand someone upTo deliberately fail to meet someone for a date or appointment.
to blow someone offTo reject or dismiss someone rudely; to ignore or cancel on them.
to be torn betweenTo be unable to choose between two options or loyalties.
to be eaten aliveTo be completely overwhelmed or destroyed (figuratively).
insurance fraudThe illegal act of deceiving an insurance company for financial gain.
to miss out (on)To lose an opportunity or fail to experience something enjoyable.
