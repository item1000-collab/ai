📚 Welcome, Archivist. Deep beneath the Winter Palace of Television, behind frost-patterned glass, the crown jewel of 1990s broadcasting sleeps inside a set of imperial eggs.

Your mission is honorable and clear: open each egg, restore each plot, and master the English vocabulary of the most-watched night in series history.
