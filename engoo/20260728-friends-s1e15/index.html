📖 Lesson summary

First paragraph. This lesson analyzed Friends S1E15 through the lens of “three disasters” — Monica’s ruined culinary audition, Chandler’s inescapable corporate loop, and Ross’s awkward romantic failures. The student, Anatoly, explored 90s sitcom humor, slang (stoned, bails), and the contrast between expectation and reality. Key vocabulary such as gourmet, eclectic, eavesdrop, aptitude and the acronym W.E.N.U.S. were discussed within the context of the episode’s cultural satire.

Second paragraph. Through role‑play and visual slides, the class examined how each character’s “disaster” delivers comedic karma. Chandler’s promotion arc was highlighted as the most engaging, while Ross’s monkey Marcel and the “dirty talk” coaching session provided rich material for discussing American sitcom tropes. The session reinforced the use of slang and corporate jargon, and the student practiced spontaneous dialogue, demonstrating a strong grasp of the episode’s cultural references and narrative structure.
