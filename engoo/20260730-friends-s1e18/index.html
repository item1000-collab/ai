Advanced vocabulary & idioms

pot calling the kettle black
Accusing someone of a flaw you also have; hypocrisy.
call someone’s bluff
Challenge an empty threat or a lie, forcing them to prove it.
lousy tips
Very small or poor gratuities; also means “bad” or “miserable”.
cursed economy
“Cursed” = seemingly under a spell; everything goes wrong economically.
mother ship calling you home
Metaphor for returning to one’s true passion or origin (fashion for Rachel).
trash talk
Insults or boastful remarks meant to intimidate opponents.
