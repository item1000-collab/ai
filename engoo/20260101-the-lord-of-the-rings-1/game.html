FELLOWSHIP ARCADE

Fifteen frames of film · sixteen words of power · five trials between the hearth and the shadow

A small golden ring leaves a warm hole in the ground and drags nine companions across a cold, wide world. The lesson deck traced that journey frame by frame; this arcade lets you walk it in mini-games drawn from the very same lines.

  • 🕹️ Play the levels in any order — the whole map is open from the start.
  • ⭐ A flawless level earns 3 stars; one or two mistakes earn 2; more earn 1.
  • 💾 Best scores are saved in your browser — leave a level at any moment, nothing is lost.
  • 🔊 Sound is synthesized live with the Web Audio API — mute it from the console above.

Keyboard: Tab move · Enter confirm · ← REAL · → DELUSION · Esc back to map
