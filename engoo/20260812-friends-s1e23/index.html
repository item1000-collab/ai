🎯 Advanced vocabulary & idioms

co‑parenting rivalry
Ongoing competition or tension between two people who share parenting responsibilities (here Ross and Susan).
to undermine
To weaken someone’s confidence or authority; to put them down. “Ross used rhetorical questions to undermine Susan.”
jab
A quick, sharp verbal attack or punch; in dialogue, a biting remark. “Ross’s rhetorical jab at Susan.”
flex (as in showing off)
To boast or display something proudly, often material possessions. “Quartz watch flex — Ross and Susan show off their timepieces.”
unlikely midwife
An unexpected person who helps with childbirth; Joey becomes a temporary birth partner for Lydia.
maiden pact
An agreement made between friends (often about marriage) — Chandler’s joke about a pact if they’re not married by 40.
