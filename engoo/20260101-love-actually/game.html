A film-based English game · Love Actually (2003)

Love, Actually:
The Mosaic Arcade

Ten interlocking love stories, one Heathrow arrivals hall, and a deck full of beautiful English. The lesson became five playable reels — complete them in any order you like, because love, actually, has no fixed sequence.

  • Free order. Every reel is unlocked from the start — hop between storylines freely and leave any reel via the map button.
  • Stars. No mistakes earns three stars; one or two earns two; more than that still earns one. Best scores are saved per reel.
  • The finale. Finish all five reels — in any order — and the Final button opens the arrivals hall.
  • Sound. Every note is synthesized live. Mute anytime with the speaker button.
