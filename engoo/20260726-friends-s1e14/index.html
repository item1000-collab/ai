Friends · Season 1 · Episode 14 (Valentine’s Day)

Three plots · double date · first date in 9 years · cleansing ritual

📺 Thematic blocks

🔄 The double date trap

Joey tricks Chandler into a blind date, but the plan backfires when Chandler's date turns out to be his ex, Janice. Chandler orders overpriced champagne on Joey’s credit card as revenge.

💔 The ultimate bedmate reveal

After drinking champagne, Chandler and Janice discover genuine chemistry. He wakes up filled with dread – he slept with his ex and has to break up with her on Valentine’s Day. Janice is completely unfazed and leaves him custom candy hearts, claiming that Chandler subconsciously keeps seeking her out.

🍣 The hibachi disaster (Ross’s first date)

Ross goes on his first date in nine years. He’s painfully awkward, especially when his ex-wife Carol appears. He tries to hide, but his date discovers Carol is pregnant with his baby. She quietly leaves – the date dies in real time.

🔥 Cleansing ritual & fire escape

Monica, Rachel, and Phoebe hold a “cleansing ritual” – they burn items from past relationships: a tearful letter, a dinner receipt, and a photo of a “hairy” ex. The fire escalates when Rachel throws in Italian grappa, and firefighters arrive – but the girls end up meeting three handsome firefighters.

💌 Candy hearts & closure

Janice leaves a box of custom candy hearts that say things like “You love me” and “Subconsciously you seek me.” She may be delusional – or she might be right. Meanwhile, Ross and Carol share a kiss, but Carol makes it clear that a reunion isn’t possible. Ross struggles with closure.

📌 Summary · Part 1
In this Valentine’s Day episode, three separate storylines weave together romance, awkwardness, and friendship. Chandler is tricked into a double date that lands him back with his eccentric ex Janice; Ross endures a painfully uncomfortable first date that crashes when his pregnant ex-wife appears; and the three women hold a cleansing ritual that literally goes up in flames. Each character faces the ghosts of past relationships, but the episode keeps its signature comedic tone.

📌 Summary · Part 2
The episode explores the messiness of moving on – Chandler’s fear of commitment, Ross’s lingering hope, and the girls’ attempt to burn away old memories. Janice, in her uniquely optimistic way, claims Chandler will always come back to her, while Ross realises that closure isn’t always neat. Ultimately, the fire brings the group together (and even introduces three firefighters), reminding us that friendship – and a little chaos – makes Valentine’s Day memorable.
