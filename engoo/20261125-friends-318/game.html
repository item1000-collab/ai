English Through TV · Level Arcade

The One with the Hypnosis Tape

Five mini-games built from one sitcom episode. Each level teaches a different skill: idioms in context, sentence judgment, comprehension, meaning matching, and a timed round that mixes everything you have already learned.

  • Fill in the blanks. Click a blank, then the word that completes the sentence.
  • True or false. Ten minutes per card. Use the arrow keys, read the explanation, then press NEXT.
  • Multiple choice. One attempt per question, then a character reacts.
  • Match the meaning. Pair each English word with its meaning.
  • Timed bonus. 750 seconds, questions from every earlier topic.

All levels are open from the start. Play in any order, leave at any time, and replay any reel to raise your stars.
