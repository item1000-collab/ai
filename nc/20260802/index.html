🧠 Lesson plan · thematic blocks

⚡ Geopolitics & defence logistics

block 1 NATO’s “roundabout” – Germany as a logistics hub; bureaucratic obstruction, state-level permits, and the Spannungsfall (state of tension) requiring a two‑thirds majority. Emergency laws vs. federal state autonomy.

🔥 Hybrid warfare & political fragmentation

block 2 Kremlin strategy: exploiting internal political cracks, using pro‑Kremlin actors to congest reinforcement networks. AfD’s links to Moscow, sanctions skepticism, and the “firewall” dilemma.

🛡️ Air defense & military overstretch

block 3 Iran’s drone‑missile swarm tactics; depletion of Patriot/THAAD interceptors; U.S. forced to prioritise threats. Crowdsourced intelligence, Russian targeting support, and casualty undercounts.

🎯 Advanced vocabulary & idioms (5+ expressions)

bureaucratic obstructionism Deliberate use of administrative procedures to slow or block action.
to bog down To hinder or delay progress; to become stuck in a process.
hybrid fashion Combination of conventional and unconventional methods (cyber, sabotage, political pressure).
casualty cover-up Deliberate concealment or minimisation of the number of injured/killed personnel.
to circumvent Find a way around (an obstacle or rule), often bypassing authority.
“Nato’s roundabout” Nickname for Germany due to its central role in logistical transit for the alliance.
to muster (a majority) To gather or assemble (support, votes) for a purpose.

Multiple U.S. bases across the Middle East have suffered “significant” damage, with U.S. forces reportedly allowing some missiles through if they don’t endanger lives. The piece highlights Iranian targeting capabilities, Russian intelligence support, and the Pentagon’s disputed casualty figures – a stark illustration of hybrid warfare and logistical vulnerability.
