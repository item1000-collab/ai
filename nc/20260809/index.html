📋 Two‑paragraph summary

Reading 1 · Yen & US leverage

The US‑Japan joint intervention to prop up the yen reveals deep unease in Washington about Tokyo’s potential to repatriate its vast US Treasury holdings. With Japan’s economy struggling under low productivity and inflation, the weak yen is both a symptom and a risk. The US needs Japanese capital to keep bond yields low, giving Japan unusual leverage — a card it may be playing through subtle policy shifts and crypto liberalisation.

Reading 2 · Climate & El Niño

Although 2026 feels exceptionally hot, it is not yet a record year. But with a powerful El Niño building and oceans at unprecedented temperatures, scientists expect 2026 to challenge the 2024 record — and 2027 to surpass it decisively. This summer’s extremes, from wildfires to heatwaves, are primarily driven by human‑caused warming, not El Niño, and they are a stark foretaste of accelerating climate disruption.

📌 Both articles highlight hidden leverage: financial (Japan) and climatic (warming acceleration).
